//////////////////////////////////
//DESCRIPTION
//////////////////////////////////
Charname&Co get an invitation to a relaxing retreat on an island about a day’s journey from Baldur’s Gate city. Little do they know that all the guests are connected in an important way, and soon they realize that they are all trapped there on the island, caught like flies in a web.

Meant to be done in Chapter 5.

Inspired by Agatha Christie’s writing.


//////////////////////////////////
//WHO TO BRING
//////////////////////////////////
Safana and Skie can talk to the NPCs, and the NPCs will have different dialogue than if Charname talks to them.

A few other companions, like Ajantis and Jaheira, have interjections.

Recommended Evil Party for maximum content: Charname, Safana, Skie, Xzar, Dorn, Edwin
Recommended Good Party for maximum content: Charname, Safana, Skie, Ajantis, Jaheira, ?????
Recommended Nuetral Party for maximum content: Charname, Safana, Skie, Garrick, Quayle, ????

Everyone else has 0-1 interjection(s)

/////////////////////
//WALKTHROUGH
/////////////////////
1. Speak to the Messenger outside of the Elfsong Tavern in Baldur's Gate city
2. Go speak to Shipmaster Adrik by the Harbormaster's building
3. Go hang out with people, tell Tenadar that you'd like a drink.
4. Go to bed with your buddy
5. Return to the Main Hut after resting
6. Find certain rhyme on a certain dead body
7. Make a decision
8. Events unfold
9. Kill the murderer
10. Talk to the Flaming Fist to return to Baldur's Gate
